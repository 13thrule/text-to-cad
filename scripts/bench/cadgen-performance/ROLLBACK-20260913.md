`8d83fb77b`. Render
stills and video frames now downsample the complete drawing buffer to requested
output dimensions, then draw labels at output resolution. Ordinary unlabeled
1× captures retain their direct canvas-encoding path.

Persistent browser reuse was deferred: it is an independent but substantial
daemon/transport/lifecycle change, about 3,600 added lines. Native tessellation,
new mesh/export products, STEP completion changes and the replacement document
store were also left on the recovery branch. No Python runtime change is layered
onto the stable checkpoint.

The stable base had one stale test asserting model-record schema 5 even though
its runtime already uses schema 6. The test now expects 6 and verifies rejection
of both retired schemas 4 and 5. No store version or runtime behavior was changed.
